Questions

Why is there a limit at all?
Reading five hundred filings a day costs money, and the alternative to a subscription is advertising, which on a finance site means being paid by whoever wants you to buy something. A price you can see is cleaner than one you cannot.
Does the count reset?
No, and companies you have already opened stay open for good. Taking back something you have already read would be worse than never showing it.
Is this investment advice?
No. It is a reading of public filings with the arithmetic shown, and the method page lists its own blind spots at length. Nobody here is a licensed adviser, and nothing on the site is a recommendation to buy or sell.
Where does the data come from?
Company filings at SEC EDGAR, and market data from Finnhub. Rebuilt every weekday evening after the American close.
Can I get a refund?
Yes. Email and it is returned, no argument, no retention script.
