What IDEX Corporation says it does

Overview IDEX Corporation ("IDEX," the "Company," "we," or "our") is a global applied solutions provider serving niche markets with mission critical components for everyday life. Substantially all of the Company’s business activities are carried out through over 50 wholly-owned subsidiaries with shared values of Trust, Team and Excellence. IDEX’s diverse family of businesses is committed to making trusted solutions that improve lives and is innovative and inquisitive in its quest to solve customers’ most challenging applied technology problems. These businesses operate with a high degree of autonomy, yet are all united by employing The IDEX Difference, a philosophy of great teams who embrace the 8020 principle while remaining hyper-focused on serving customers.
