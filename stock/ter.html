The case for TER

  • 26% of revenue drops through to net profit.
  • Earnings per share up 152.8%.

The case against

  • Pricey at 54.1× earnings, against a long-run market average nearer 20×.
  • Priced at 13.9× sales, which leaves no room for a stumble.
  • Swings harder than the market (beta 1.70).
  • Priced at 21× book value. Very little hard asset backing here.
  • Free cash flow is only 0.7% of its market value, a thin cash return for the price.
