The case for KO

  • Earns 43% back on shareholder equity.
  • 29% of revenue drops through to net profit.
  • Pays a 3.1% dividend while you wait.
  • Gross margin of 62% absorbs cost shocks.
  • Moves less than the market (beta 0.30).

The case against

  • Priced at 7.5× sales with revenue growing only 6.5%.
  • Only 40% of FY2025's $13.1B profit arrived as free cash.
  • Free cash flow is only 1.4% of its market value, a thin cash return for the price.
