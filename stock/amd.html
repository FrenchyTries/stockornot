The case for AMD

  • Revenue up 39.5% on the year.
  • Generated $6.7B of free cash flow in FY2025, 19% of revenue.
  • Holds more cash ($5.5B) than long-term debt ($2.3B).
  • Earnings per share up 124.3%.
  • Has compounded revenue at 28.8% a year over five years.
  • Current assets cover the near-term bills 2.6 times over.

The case against

  • Very expensive at 157.6× earnings. Years of growth are already in the price.
  • Priced at 24.5× sales, which leaves no room for a stumble.
  • Swings harder than the market (beta 2.53).
  • Free cash flow is only 0.7% of its market value, a thin cash return for the price.
