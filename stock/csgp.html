The non-GAAP financial measures that we may disclose include EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA margin, Adjusted Net Income, and Adjusted EPS. EBITDA is our net income (loss) before interest income or expense, net, other expense or income, net, loss on debt extinguishment, income taxes, depreciation and amortization.
