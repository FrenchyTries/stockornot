("we," "us," "our", "Lumentum" or the "Company") is a global leader in optical and photonic technologies and an industry-leading provider of optical and photonic products based on revenue and market share. We operate in one reportable segment as a single, integrated enterprise. See "Note 17. Operating Segments and Geographic Information" to the consolidated financial statements. We disaggregate revenue by type of product, which are Components and Systems, and by geography.
