What Public Service Enterprise Group says it does

—Regulatory Issues—Federal Regulation. As a result of the capacity market price increases, the costs of which are flowed through to customers, and per direction to EDCs from the BPU, PSE&G filed a petition in May 2025 that provided proposals to mitigate bill impacts to customers. In June 2025, the BPU approved a settlement under which PSE&G applied a credit to each residential electric customer’s monthly bill for July 2025 and August 2025, with the offset being charged on monthly bills for September 2025 through February 2026. PSE&G agreed to waive carrying costs on the outstanding credit amount.
