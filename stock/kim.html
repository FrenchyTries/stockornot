The case for KIM

  • Generated $1.1B of free cash flow in FY2025, 51% of revenue.
  • 28% of revenue drops through to net profit.
  • Free cash flow of 7.3% of its market value a year: a lot of cash for the price.

  • Has compounded revenue at 15.1% a year over five years.
  • Gross margin of 69% absorbs cost shocks.

The case against

  • Long-term debt of $7.7B would take 7 years of operating cash flow to repay.
  • Priced at 6.9× sales with revenue growing only 4.4%.
  • Growth is slowing: revenue up 4.4% this year against 15.1% a year over five.
  • Return on equity of only 6%.
  • Dividend takes 120% of earnings, leaving little cushion.
  • The price trend is weak (42/100): +2.7% over a year, −7.5% over three months, 15% below its 52-week high.
