What CRH says it does

Overview CRH is the leading provider of building materials critical to modernizing infrastructure. With our team of 83,032 people across 3,961 locations, our unmatched scale, connected portfolio, and deep local relationships make us the partner of choice for transportation, water and reindustrialization projects, shaping communities for a better tomorrow. The Company, which has market leadership positions in North America, Europe and Australia, has grown rapidly since formation in 1970 and in 2025 generated $37.4 billion of Total revenues, $3.8 billion of Net income and $7.7 billion of Adjusted EBITDA*. 1 In 2025, 40% of Total revenues came from infrastructure, 32% from residential construction and 28% from non-residential construction. 60% of Total revenues came from sales to new-build construction, while 40% of Total revenues came from repair and remodel activity.
