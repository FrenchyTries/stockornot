Privacy

What this site keeps about you, and where.

No advertising, no analytics and no tracking scripts. What is kept is what the cart and the alerts need in order to work, and this page lists all of it.

Last updated October 7, 2026.

Without an account

Nothing about you leaves your browser. The cart, with any notes and planned amounts, the cards you have already swiped past, and which companies you have opened (for the free allowance) are kept in your browser's own storage on this device. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it.

With an account

Signing in asks for an email address and sends a one-time sign-in link or code to it. There is no password. With an account, these are kept in the site's database, hosted by Supabase:

  • Your email address, to sign you in and, if you ask for them, to send earnings alerts.
  • That you agreed to the terms: which version, and when, recorded as the account is created.
  • Your cart: the companies in it, when you added them and at what price, your notes and planned amounts, and a record of the last order sent for each, so a retry cannot place it twice.
  • Your alert settings, and a log of which alerts have been sent, so no report is emailed twice.
  • Membership status, once membership opens. Card details never reach this site; the payment provider handles them.

The database only lets you read or change your own rows. That rule is enforced by the database itself, so it holds even against a request crafted by hand.

Earnings alerts

Alerts are off until you switch them on. When they are on, an email goes out through Resend a week before a company in your cart reports, listing the companies and what the street expects. Turn them off at any time under Account, then Earnings alerts, with the link at the bottom of every alert, or by replying "unsubscribe".

A brokerage connection

If you connect an Alpaca account, the keys or sign-in token are encrypted into a cookie in your browser that only this site's server can read. They are not stored in any database here. The cookie expires after 30 days, or at once when you disconnect. Orders you confirm go straight to Alpaca, whose own privacy policy covers your account there.

Who else is involved

  • Vercel hosts the site. Like any web host it keeps short-lived request logs, which include IP addresses.
  • Supabase holds accounts and the data listed above, and sends the sign-in emails.
  • Resend sends earnings alerts, and only to people who switched them on.
  • GitHub runs the nightly job that sends the alerts. While it runs it reads the email address, cart and alert settings of people who switched alerts on, and keeps none of them.
  • Alpaca, only if you connect a brokerage account.
  • Finnhub supplies market data and news. Its requests come from this site's server and carry nothing about you.

These are the only services that handle anything about you. Nothing is sold, rented, shared for marketing, or used to train AI models. The typeface and the sign-in library are served from this site, not from a font service or a code CDN.

Cookies and browser storage

The site sets one cookie, and only if you connect a brokerage account: the sealed connection described above. Your cart, the cards you have swiped, your scoring style and, once you sign in, your session are kept in your browser's own storage, because the app cannot work without them. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and nothing optional, so there is nothing to accept or refuse and no cookie banner.

Getting a copy, and deleting it

Signed in, the Account tab has Download my data, a file with everything kept about you: the account, the saved cart with its notes and amounts, the alert settings and the alerts sent. Without an account, Export CSV in the cart gives you the cart, which is all there is.

The Account tab also has Delete my account. It asks once more, then deletes the account, the email address and everything attached to it at once, for good, and disconnects this browser from any brokerage account. You can also empty the cart and switch alerts off at any time, and signing out removes the session from that browser. If anything here does not work, write to hello@stockornot.com from the account's address: a copy or a deletion is done within 30 days, and you get a reply when it is.

Do Not Track, and children

There is nothing here to track you with: no advertising, no analytics and no third-party scripts, so every visitor is treated the way a browser's Do Not Track signal asks, whether or not it is sent. StockOrNot is meant for adults; it is not for anyone under 18, and it does not knowingly keep information about children under 13.

Changes

If what is kept changes, this page changes first, and the date at the top moves.
